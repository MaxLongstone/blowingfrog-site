China invented the format. A full storyline — romance, betrayal, revenge, usually in that order, sometimes simultaneously — broken into 60 to 90 episodes of about 90 seconds each. Vertical. Designed for a phone. Designed to make you watch the next one before you've consciously decided to.

The hooks are aggressively stupid in the best possible way. "My Billionaire Boss Recognized My Face." "I Married the Wrong Twin." "My Son's Ventriloquist Doll Is Leading a Latin American Revolution." They sound like they were written by an algorithm that only ever read airport paperbacks and one Telenovela synopsis. Except they work. Seventy million monthly active users on ReelShort alone. Ninety percent from the US.

Fox Entertainment bought a stake. Telemundo adapted telenovelas. SAG-AFTRA signed vertical agreements. When Hollywood starts paying union rates to protect people from a format, the format has already won.

And now we're in.

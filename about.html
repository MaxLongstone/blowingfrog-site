About Us
We Are A Visual Creative
Agency.

Blowing Frog was born from a midnight session of friends, beers, and odd trivia of a frog eating a firecracker. From that jamboree we started cooking, and cooking with the idea that brands needed to play the game with some teeth, that marketing had been deranged, that there was a lot of bark, but no bite.

We were tired of bland campaigns, safe designs, and brands that got lost in the noise. So we leapt into the unknown and created a studio that thrives on weird, wild, and wonderful ideas.

Our team is a diverse group of creatives, tech wizards, and marketing rebels who come together with one goal—to make your brand ribbit louder than the rest. We're not just here to follow trends; we're here to create them.

Every leap we take is backed by a commitment to innovation, collaboration, and results. Whether we're crafting an eye-popping animation, optimizing a brand for SEO dominance, or creating a sound that sends chills down spines, we approach every project with the same energy and passion that made us leap in the first place.

At Blowing Frog, our values are simple: Be bold. Stay weird. Always leap forward – and, no matter what happens, never say no. There's no task, no matter how outlandish you can't pull off. You just need the right sherpa... or the proper frog. We don't just deliver—we disrupt, innovate, and transform. And we're not afraid to get a little messy in the process.

Somebody at Runway made a beautiful little film about a lamp.

Good film. No sarcasm, no hedging. Lighting like a Vermeer that got a job in retail, timing that lands, the whole thing sitting in that spot where you stop counting fingers and start caring. That is the trick, and they pulled it off.

And my first thought — my very first, unfiltered, straight-from-the-lizard-cortex thought — was:

That thing is going to murder a family in Ohio.

Not "wow, the tech matured." Not "the pipeline has shifted." Nope. Murder. Ohio. A lamp, dragging its cord across a shag carpet at 3 a.m. like a sommelier of bad intentions.

STOP.

Pull the jalopy over. Kill the engine. Because before we go tearing off into the swamp I owe you the setup, and the setup is twenty-four years old and it involves a Swedish man telling America to get a grip.

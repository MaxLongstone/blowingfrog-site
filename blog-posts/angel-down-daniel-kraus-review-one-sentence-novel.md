Izzard framing (dosage / saturation) as thesis for Angel Down's difficulty. Setup (five morally compromised men + an angel in Meuse-Argonne barbed wire), Cyril Bagger as the right narrator for a book about remaining human. Meuse-Argonne context: deadliest campaign in US history, more graves at Romagne than Omaha Beach. Core critical move: form found, not imposed — Kraus found the theme (warfare as a self-replicating wheel) and built the form to replicate it. Original insight: Kraus multiplies where every writer is taught to subtract; the absence of periods removes the reader's exit, so accumulation becomes duration not decoration — and this is the move that makes the visceral passages land rather than embarrass. Negative case stated at full strength (Objective Standard); answered with a falsifiable test (put periods in, the book loses the argument). Practical reading guide: longer sits, Izzard rule (saturation is a feature not a failure). Ends on an open comma, on purpose, in the last line.
